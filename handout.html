Crib Guard

See what they feel. Hear what they need.
CpE 191 / EEE 193 — Senior Design
Sacramento State · Spring 2026
Team: John Hammer · Sam Hammer · Ryan Huston · Jasen Pritchard
Crib Guard is a smart infant monitor that goes beyond a live video feed. A thermal camera quietly watches for the temperature signature of a wet diaper, an on-device audio model recognizes the difference between a fussy whimper and an actual cry, and the caregiver sees and hears it all on a touchscreen across the room — with an audible alert the moment something needs attention.

1 Thermal Wetness Detection

A FLIR Lepton 3.5 thermal sensor watches the crib for the cooling signature of a wet diaper. Caregivers draw a region of interest directly on the live feed to focus detection on the diaper area, and the system triggers a visual banner plus a two-tone audio alert through the parent unit's speaker.

2 Smart Cry Detection

A real-time audio classifier on the baby unit distinguishes infant cries from ambient room sound using pitch, spectral flatness, and persistence scoring. It's lullaby-aware: when soothing music is playing, the state machine won't mistake it for crying.

3 Live Camera + Listen Mode

Low-latency thermal video streams over a dedicated WiFi link to the parent display. A push-to-talk "Listen" toggle pipes the baby unit's microphone to the parent's speaker via Opus over RTP for clean, low-delay audio.

4 Touchscreen Control Panel

The parent unit runs a custom kiosk UI built on LVGL with a 7" DSI touchscreen. Caregivers can view the live feed, set the wetness detection region, toggle listen mode, play lullabies on the baby unit, and acknowledge alerts — all from one screen.
